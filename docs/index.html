What is Recap?

A simple Chrome extension for LeetCode users who want to stay consistent with daily problem solving.

Recap checks the LeetCode Daily Challenge and reminds you when it is time to solve it. The extension shows the challenge's difficulty, topics, completion status and streak from your browser toolbar.

Everything you need to stay consistent

Recap keeps the Daily Challenge visible without adding unnecessary complexity.

Daily Challenge

See today's LeetCode Daily Challenge together with its difficulty and topic tags.

Completion Status

Quickly see whether you have completed today's challenge.

Daily Reminders

Get Chrome notifications at the reminder time you choose.

Smart Retries

Choose 15, 30, 60 or 120 minute retry intervals until the challenge is solved.

LeetCode Streak

Keep track of your LeetCode streak and stay motivated to solve problems consistently.

Codeforces Tracking

Add your Codeforces handle in settings to track your Codeforces streak as well.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Recap Chrome extension.

What does Recap do?

Recap reminds you to solve the LeetCode Daily Challenge and shows the challenge's completion status, difficulty, topics and streak.

Does Recap send LeetCode reminders?

Yes. Recap uses Chrome notifications at your chosen time and can retry reminders until the Daily Challenge is completed.

Do I need a LeetCode account?

Yes. You must be signed in to LeetCode in Chrome to see your challenge status and streak.

Does Recap track Codeforces?

Yes. Add your Codeforces handle in Recap's settings to see your Codeforces streak.

Is Recap free and open source?

Yes. Recap is free and open source. The source code is available on GitHub .

How do I install Recap?

Download Recap and follow the installation guide to install it using Chrome's Load unpacked option.

Recap is an open-source project developed and maintained on GitHub. You can inspect the source code, report issues and contribute to the project.
