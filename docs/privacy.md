# Recap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

## What Recap reads

- Your `LEETCODE_SESSION` cookie, used only to authenticate requests to leetcode.com.
- Your LeetCode Daily Challenge status, username, and streak, returned by LeetCode.
- If enabled, your public Codeforces handle and submission data from the Codeforces API.

## What Recap stores

Your settings (reminder time, retry interval, notification choice, Codeforces handle) and reminder state are stored locally in your browser with the Chrome Storage API.

## What Recap does not do

VERIFY against the source code before publishing: Recap does not send your cookie or data to any server other than LeetCode and Codeforces, and does not include analytics or advertising.

## Notifications and alarms

Chrome notifications and alarms are used only to schedule and show reminders. You can disable them in Settings.
