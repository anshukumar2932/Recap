# Recap FAQ: LeetCode Daily Challenge Chrome Extension

## What is Recap?
A free Chrome extension that reminds you to solve the LeetCode Daily Challenge and shows completion status and streaks.

## Does Recap send LeetCode reminders?
Yes. It checks at your chosen time and retries every 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes until you finish.

## Can Recap show whether I solved today's LeetCode problem?
Yes. It reads your LeetCode user status and displays completion.

## Does Recap show my LeetCode streak?
Yes, in the popup navbar.

## Does Recap track Codeforces?
Yes. Since v0.2.1 you can enable Codeforces in Settings and add your handle to see your solving streak.

## Do I need a LeetCode account?
Yes. You must be signed in to leetcode.com for status, username, and streak.

## Is Recap free and open source?
Yes, under the MIT license.

## Is it safe?
Recap uses your LeetCode session cookie only to request your own data from LeetCode. See [privacy](privacy.md) and [permissions](permissions.md).

## Which browsers work?
Google Chrome and Chromium browsers with Manifest V3 support.

## Does it replace LeetCode?
No. It is a lightweight companion for the Daily Challenge.
